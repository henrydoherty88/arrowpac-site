OPERATING PROCEDURES

Shipping with Arrowpac: how it works

STEP 01

Arrange freight pickup

  • Call your local Arrowpac terminal (see Network page)
  • Or call central dispatch at 800-222-2003
  • Same-day LTL pickup available from most origins
STEP 02

Prepare Domestic Bill-of-Lading

  • Show Arrowpac as consignee (ocean shipments)
  • Show ultimate consignee with physical address + phone
  • Prepaid / collect — to destination, to port, or other arrangement
  • Indicate insurance requirement (if any)
STEP 03

Attach required documentation

  • Copy of commercial invoice
  • Shipper Export Declaration (SED)
  • Consignee Tax I.D. Number
  • Banking requirements (if applicable)
STEP 04

Track, discharge, deliver

  • Track shipment via APAC bill-of-lading number (Track page)
  • Discharged at San Juan (Cataño) or DR port
  • Daily islandwide delivery in PR via Cataño warehouse
  • Customized email reporting for every shipment
