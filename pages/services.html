Ocean LTL & FCL

Weekly sailings · 5 U.S. terminals · Door-to-door

The workhorse service. Less-than-truckload and full-container-load ocean freight from five U.S. consolidation terminals to Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic. Same-day LTL pickup. Weekly departures. Through bills-of-lading handle everything from pickup to final delivery.

  • ModesLTL · LCL · FCL · Reefer
  • OriginsEast Windsor NJ · Jacksonville FL · Miami FL · Chicago (Joliet IL) · Los Angeles (Pico Rivera CA)
  • DestinationsSan Juan, PR (Cataño) · Dominican Republic · Caribbean Islands
  • Transit to PRJacksonville 3 days · Miami 4 days · East Windsor 7 days · Chicago 7 days · Los Angeles 12 days
  • Cut-offsTypically 1 day before sailing — see Sailing Schedule for terminal-specific cut-offs
  • DocumentationThrough bill-of-lading · Shipper Export Declaration handled · customs brokerage in-house
